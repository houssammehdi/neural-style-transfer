# Credits and licences

Every image in this directory was generated by this repository from the inputs below, on CPU,
with the original Caffe VGG-19 weights. The inputs themselves are never committed:
[`scripts/fetch_examples.py`](../../scripts/fetch_examples.py) downloads them from pinned commits
and checks the SHA-256 digests listed here.

## Paintings (style images)

All four are reproductions of two-dimensional artworks whose copyright has expired. Faithful
photographic reproductions of such works do not attract a new copyright in the United States
(*Bridgeman Art Library v. Corel Corp.*, 1999) or in the EU (Article 14 of Directive (EU)
2019/790). The files come from the example inputs of
[jcjohnson/neural-style](https://github.com/jcjohnson/neural-style) (commit
[`07c4b82`](https://github.com/jcjohnson/neural-style/tree/07c4b8299f8fbdafec0c514fc820ff1d7ff62e46/examples/inputs));
that repository does not record where it obtained the reproductions.

| File | Work | Artist (died) | Status | SHA-256 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `starry_night.jpg` | *The Starry Night*, 1889 | Vincent van Gogh (1890) | Public domain worldwide | `0592ab0f7b51…` |
| `the_scream.jpg` | *The Scream*, 1893 | Edvard Munch (1944) | Public domain in the US (published before 1931) and wherever the term is life + 80 years or shorter | `f3075cddeb97…` |
| `shipwreck.jpg` | *The Shipwreck*, 1805 | J. M. W. Turner (1851) | Public domain worldwide | `4f7cf34e3cd4…` |
| `woman-with-hat-matisse.jpg` | *Woman with a Hat*, 1905 | Henri Matisse (1954) | Public domain in the US (published before 1931) and, since 1 January 2025, wherever the term is life + 70 years (EU, UK, Norway); still protected where it is longer, e.g. Mexico (life + 100) | `6a8b2383d9c1…` |

Gatys et al. (2016) use *The Shipwreck* too, captioned "The Shipwreck of the Minotaur", in the
figure that shows one photograph in several styles. Works by artists who died after 1954 (Picasso, Escher, Kahlo, ...) and photographs of
people, both also among jcjohnson's examples, are deliberately not used.

The licences are the ones scikit-image states in the docstrings of `skimage.data`
(`skimage/data/_fetchers.py`), quoted here. (`skimage/data/README.txt` at that tag does not list

a portrait of a real person. Full digests are pinned in `scripts/fetch_examples.py`.

## Masks

The masks of the spatial-control examples are computed from the photographs by
[`scripts/gallery.py`](../../scripts/gallery.py): a horizon line at 75.5 % of the image height for
the rocket, and a colour segmentation of the red-and-white porcelain for the coffee cup.

## Network weights

The images were made with the VGG-19 weights of Karen Simonyan and Andrew Zisserman, *Very Deep
Convolutional Networks for Large-Scale Image Recognition* (ICLR 2015), in the Keras conversion by
François Chollet
([fchollet/deep-learning-models, release v0.1](https://github.com/fchollet/deep-learning-models/releases/tag/v0.1)).
That repository states that these weights are "ported from the ones released by VGG at Oxford
under the Creative Commons Attribution License"
([CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)).
